TOBIRA World　How to Play

A life board game of money and choices: you are born somewhere in the world and live 29 years, from age 6 to 35.
You don't choose where you're born. Same dice, same board for everyone — but what each player can see is different. You'll find out why after the game.

👥 2–6 players ⏱ ~25 min + reflection 🖥 one shared screen

🎒 What you need

  • 🖥One computer or tablet with a browser (a screen big enough for everyone works best). Open the game page and you're ready.
  • 🙌2–6 players. Tokens and money live on the screen — no extra pieces needed.

🏁 The goal

The ranking isn't decided by money, but by ♥ Happiness.
You earn ♥ by opening life's Doors. In other words,
♥ = how often you chose with your own will. Money and ★ Learning are the keys that make it possible.

🏠 Getting started IMPORTANT

1

Enter your names and press START!

2

One at a time, secretly flip your Family Card. It shows where you're born, your starting money, base pay, and your strength.

3

Don't show it to anyone! What family you were born into stays a secret until the end.

🔁 On your turn

1

Roll the dice (just press the button).

2

Your token moves that many squares.

3

Follow the square you land on. When choices appear, read them aloud and discuss with the table if you like — but the decision is yours!

4

Then it's the next player's turn. Repeat until everyone reaches the goal.

🧒 Childhood rule (age 6–15)

The first few squares use no dice. Press "🧒 Step forward" to move one square at a time, stopping on every square.
These are the years that build the base of a life.

🎲 The squares

💰Work

You get paid: base pay + ★ Learning × rate. As ★ grows, your job and pay change too.

💸Expense

School supplies, medical bills — living costs money.

⚡Event

Luck and accidents. Sometimes important information comes your way.

📚Learning

A chance to grow ★ Learning. Pay for it — or learn for free?

🚪Door

The glowing brown squares. A life choice arrives — opening one can earn ♥.

🌱🏁Start · Goal

Born at 6, goal at 35.

🚪 Doors & keys

Landing on a Door square shows several options. The good ones often need "keys".

🔑 Keys = 💰 Money · ★ Learning 🔒 Not enough? It won't open
  • 📚★ Learning is a future key — it opens doors and raises your pay.
  • 👁You may meet options shown only as "？？？" — invisible choices. What they really are… you'll find out after the game.

🏆 When everyone finishes PLAY TO THE END

🗣 Reflectiontalk about what you felt

Don't stop at the ranking — one game runs all the way through reflection time. Same board, so why did our lives turn out so different? Use the "Talk about it" questions on screen.

👩‍🏫 For facilitators (teachers & hosts)

⚠️The start screen has a setting to include heavy life events (loss, disaster). Switch it on or off to suit your group — it starts OFF.

🤫Make sure each Family Card is seen by its owner only. If asked what "？？？" means, we recommend not revealing it until everyone finishes.

🗣Plan 10–15 minutes for reflection. "Was it really about effort?" and "Why couldn't some doors be seen?" make strong anchors.

🌐The title screen switches between 日本語 and English, so mixed-language groups can play as they are.
