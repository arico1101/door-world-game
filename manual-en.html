TOBIRA World

A life board game of money and choices
2–6 players ~25 min one shared screen
The ranking isn't decided by money, but by ♥ Happiness.
♥ = how often you chose with your own will. Money and ★ Learning are the "keys" that make it possible.
Why age 6 to 35? — Because the base of a life is built in childhood. You don't choose where you're born. Same dice, same board for everyone — yet what each player can see is different. You'll find out why after the game.
